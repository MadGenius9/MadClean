## Registry resource and cancellation contract

The ARP adapter now uses fixed-capacity RegEnumKeyExW rather than whole subkey-name arrays, and whitelisted type/size-first RegQueryValueExW rather than unbounded value reads. Name buffers hold256 characters; display fields have fixed byte caps, flags require exact DWORD sizes, and changed/oversized/malformed responses are not retried with larger allocations. Both uninstall names receive NULL-data presence queries only. Text is literal, never expanded or interpreted as a command. Strict single-terminal-NUL decoding is a conservative policy, not a Windows guarantee. [Detailed bounds and official sources](REGISTRY-METADATA-BOUNDS.md).

A20-second cooperative worker and30-second caller wait preserve responsiveness. A single-worker gate stays held until worker-owned RegistryKeys are disposed; cancellation/timeouts cannot manufacture a complete baseline or absence verdict. Synchronous native calls may finish after the caller stops waiting; no force-close/abort is used. Other native metadata modules retain their own bounded waits and ownership gates.
